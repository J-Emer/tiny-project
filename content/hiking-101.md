Hiking is fun. Here are some basic tips!

1. Don't trip.
2. Bring extra water and snacks.
3. Watch for snakes and other animals on the trail.
4. Bring a poncho or rain jacket.
5. Have a map of the area, and a compass. 


Following these basic tips will allow you to have a fun and safe time on the trail.
