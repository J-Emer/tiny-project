The following example demonstrates the minimum front matter required in your markdown files for Tiny to render a page.

The `title` and `slug` properties are the only required fields. All other front matter properties are optional.

It is recommended to include either `nav` or `tags`, depending on how you want the page to be discovered:

* **`nav`** — Set to `true` to include the page in the site's navigation menu.
* **`tags`** — Assign one or more tags to associate the page with a category or group of related content.

Without navigation or another mechanism for linking to the page, visitors will need to know its URL to access it.
